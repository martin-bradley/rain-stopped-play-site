Common help

Starting a new match
Choose Quick Match when you want a fresh short game. If an unfinished match is saved, check whether you want to resume it before starting again.
Saved unfinished matches and Resume Match
Rain Stopped Play can keep an unfinished match locally on your device so you can come back to it later using Resume Match.
Custom teams
You can create custom clubs and playing XIs in the app. They are stored locally on your device alongside your other game data.
Championship and Series progress
Championship and five-match Series progress is saved locally as you play, so you can continue a competition without needing an account.
Restoring app data
Rain Stopped Play does not use accounts or cloud sync. Game data is stored locally on your device. Any restoration provided through an iOS device backup is handled by iOS rather than by Rain Stopped Play.
