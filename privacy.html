Rain Stopped Play respects your privacy

Rain Stopped Play does not require an account. It does not serve adverts, track users, collect personal information or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics, achievements, custom teams, playing XIs and match history are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to make the game work and to remember your progress.

Third-party data collection

Rain Stopped Play does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. No personal information is shared with third parties.
